Why Whisky

A music app that listens back.

No ads. No autoplay traps. Just a quiet curator, a clean interface and the songs you actually asked for.

Maestro, the AI curator

Ask for facts, a song's story or a playlist for the mood. Maestro replies in English or Hindi and returns real, playable tracks.

Mood mixes

Workout in Punjabi, focus in lo-fi, sleep in Hindi ambient. Ten moods, tuned to the languages you listen in.

Synced lyrics

Karaoke-grade timing that follows the player clock, with a sync offset for when a line runs a touch early.

Listening rooms

Spin up a six-letter room code, invite friends and see who is listening live. Powered by Supabase Realtime presence.

Browse offline

Saved songs keep their covers, lyrics and metadata without a connection. Audio streams when you are back online.

Personal widgets

Pin Now Playing, Top Artists, Recent Plays, Stats and Maestro tips. Drag to reorder, remove whenever you like.
